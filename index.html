Superior Craftsmanship

Exquisite Specifications

Built with precision aluminium formwork and curated with world-class materials.

Structure & Wall Finish

  • Earthquake resistant RCC structure engineered with Aluminium Formwork.
  • Floor-to-ceiling clear height of 3.35 meters (11 feet).
  • Internal walls finished with premium Gypsum / Neeru with plastic emulsion paint.
  • External facade with all-weather weather-shield exterior texture paint.

Flooring & Balconies

  • Living, Dining, Bedroom and Kitchen finished with Marble Flooring.
  • Balconies and terraces adorned with rustic wooden finish anti-skid tiles.
  • Anti-skid designer vitrified tiles in all bathrooms.
  • Full height designer wall dado tiles up to 8 feet in toilets.

Doors, Windows & Automation

  • Grand main door with biometric and digital smart security lock.
  • Premium laminated internal flush doors with mortised architectural hardware.
  • Heavy-duty powder-coated aluminium windows with sound-insulating SGU glass.
  • Adequate modular switches with concealed FRLS copper wiring.

Safety, Security & Sustainability

  • 24x7 High-definition CCTV surveillance across common campus zones.
  • Emergency alarm systems and dedicated Nurse Care Station on 2nd floor.
  • Rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment plant (STP) & organic waste converter.
  • High-speed passenger and stretcher elevators with power backup.
